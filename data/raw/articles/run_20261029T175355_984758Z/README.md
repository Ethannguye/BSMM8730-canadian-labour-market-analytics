# Scraped Labour Force Survey releases

Source: Statistics Canada, The Daily. Source URLs and retrieval timestamps are recorded in articles_index.csv and articles_index.json.

Three deliberately selected releases form a pilot sample. They do not provide complete monthly text coverage for 2019-2025.

Original HTML is preserved. TXT files contain narrative headings and paragraphs. Navigation, tables and figures are excluded. Extraction stops at source-note/related sections when identified. The HTML retains material omitted from the text.

Compare each TXT with its source before changing review_status. Keep the historical publication date separate from the reference month. Archived release figures may differ from revised CSV estimates; use the CSV for analytical calculations and the articles for context. Province and industry tags require review during preparation.
